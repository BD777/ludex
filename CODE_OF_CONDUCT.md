# Code Of Conduct

Ludex contributors are expected to keep project spaces respectful, practical,
and focused on improving the software.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate when discussing bugs, designs, and source-adapter limitations.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on reproducible facts and actionable
  proposals.
- Avoid posting private content, account data, cookies, Telegram sessions,
  downloaded source media, or other sensitive material.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or repeated disruptive behavior.
- Sharing private or sensitive data without permission.
- Using project spaces to distribute copyrighted source content or account-only
  material.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments and issues that violate
these expectations. Repeated or severe violations may lead to a ban from project
spaces.
